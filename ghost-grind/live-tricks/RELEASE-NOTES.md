# Ghost Grind — Live Tricks

The approved motion mockup is now integrated into real six-player gameplay in the existing shared half-pipe arena.

Nine moves: B kickflip, X heelflip, B+X double flip, Y 720 spin, up+Y backflip, down+Y frontflip, B+Y varial flip, L indy grab, R melon grab. Press chord buttons close together. Each move has its own duration and score; risky moves reward more, repeats earn less, and held buttons never auto-repeat. Only completed landed combos score. Late unfinished moves/chords bail and lose the pending combo.

Board flips use a separate deck, trucks and wheels; body flips keep board/rider together; 720s turn twice; grabs lean differently. Move names, pending multipliers, landing rings and LANDED/BAIL messages are live. The approved camera, illustrated arena and six portrait score pills are preserved.
