Ghost Grind for the standalone Android Home Vibe tablet and six phone browsers.

- Six skateboarders doing ollies, kickflips, heelflips, 180s, grabs and rail grinds.
- Five 90-second rounds. Each round's lowest-scoring living player becomes a ghost; one champion survives round five.
- Ghosts keep skating and can send short gusts at living rivals. Only landed tricks score; unfinished tricks bail and unlanded combos vanish at the buzzer.
- Six connected phones required; disconnects freeze the timer and simulation.

Download **Ghost-Grind-Home-Vibe.zip** and leave it compressed. On the Home Vibe tablet: **Settings / Console settings → Manage games (ADMIN) → Add a game from ZIP**, select the file, then open the imported game. Use **Connect Players** to join all six phones on the same Wi-Fi. No replacement APK or phone APK is required. Older launchers may show their standard imported-game icon; the illustrated cover is inside the game.

The complete game-source ZIP includes all game code, original artwork, tests and validation screenshots. The Android integration adds Ghost Grind as catalogue entry 50 and preserves all 49 previous entries. Source review: https://github.com/Boojabaunga2/Arcadium-Home-OS/pull/34
